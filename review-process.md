# Review Process — After Every Change

Use a reviewer subagent after every planning or coding change.

## Reviewer mandate

Check whether the change matches the user’s direction:

- Premium refined website.
- Clean, smooth, modern feel.
- Non-generic visual identity.
- Mixed real photos + 3D glass/depth UI.
- Mountains-first hero.
- Elegant editorial typography.
- Main CTA: `Discover Nepal`.
- Contact section: yes.
- 8 popular visually extraordinary Nepal places.
- Real images only.
- No hallucinated facts.
- No hotel, transport, flight, package, pricing, or booking services.

## Design QA checklist

Reject or flag if:

- It looks like a generic travel template.
- It uses default SaaS fonts without character.
- It adds too many visual effects.
- It makes 3D more important than real Nepal imagery.
- It uses fake itinerary, fake statistics, or unsupported claims.
- It includes hotel/transport/service content.
- It ignores reduced-motion support.
- It reduces readability for style.

## Subagent output format

Reviewer must return:

- Pass/fail.
- Issues.
- Required fixes.
- Questions that must be asked directly to user.
