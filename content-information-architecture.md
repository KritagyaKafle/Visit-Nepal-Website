# Content + Information Architecture

## Proposed one-page structure

### 1. Hero

Goal:

- Make Nepal feel immediate, cinematic, and desirable.

Elements:

- Glass navigation.
- Huge `NEPAL` text.
- Mountains-first real-photo composition.
- 3D glass/depth UI layer over the photo.
- Short headline.
- Primary CTA: `Discover Nepal`.
- Small place preview cards.

Copy status:

- `TBD / ask user`.

No fake facts.

### 2. About Nepal

Goal:

- Short emotional introduction.

Content constraints:

- No unsupported claims.
- No invented statistics.
- No fake itinerary.

Possible angle:

- Culture, mountains, heritage, peace, and sacred places.

Needs user approval.

### 3. Featured places

Required places from user:

- Pashupatinath.
- Swayambhu.
- Lumbini.

Recommended candidate places for approval:

- Everest / Khumbu region.
- Annapurna / Machapuchare view.
- Pokhara / Phewa Lake.
- Bhaktapur Durbar Square.
- Chitwan.

Total planned count:

- 8 places.

Status:

- Candidate list only until user confirms.

Card fields:

- Place name.
- Short approved description.
- Image.
- Mood label, e.g. sacred / heritage / sunrise / peace.

No facts until verified/approved:

- Dates.
- UNESCO claims.
- Exact location details.
- Religious claims beyond user-approved wording.

### 4. Experience moods

Purpose:

- Replace service cards from sample image with mood/promotion cards only.

Cards should promote feelings, not sell services.

Possible cards:

- Sacred mornings.
- Mountain silence.
- Heritage walks.
- Peaceful gardens.

Status:

- Needs user approval.

### 5. Smooth story section

Purpose:

- Scroll-based visual journey between Nepal places.

Important:

- Do not present as fixed itinerary unless user wants itinerary.

Better label:

- “A visual journey through Nepal.”

Motion emphasis:

- Smooth imported/borrowed component patterns may be adapted from Componentry, React Bits, and OriginKit after license/source review.

### 6. Contact

Goal:

- Let visitors express interest or ask questions.
- Not a booking form.

Elements:

- Frosted glass card over dark background.
- Heading: "Want to explore Nepal?" or similar approved copy.
- Form fields (pending final user approval):
  - Name.
  - Email.
  - Message.
- Submit button: `Send` or `Get in Touch`.
- No phone number field unless user requests.
- No pricing or package selection fields.

Status:

- Form fields proposed. User approval needed for final wording and fields.

### 7. Final CTA

Purpose:

- Encourage exploration, not booking.

CTA:

- `Discover Nepal`.
- Do not use `Book`.

### 8. Footer

Elements:

- Low-contrast glass style.
- Repeat navigation links.
- Small social media icons.
- Copyright text.
- No service links (hotels, flights, etc.).
